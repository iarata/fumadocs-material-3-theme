The sidebar groups related pages together. Open **Theme guide**, then **Components**, to explore another level of navigation.

## Nested pages

Select a group to expand or collapse it. Select **Overview** to open its landing page. Top-level pages and groups show their icon; pages inside a group are listed without icons on a guide line, aligned under the group name. The current page has a tinted background and a stronger label.

## Keyboard navigation

Use **Tab** to focus the sidebar, then **↑** and **↓** to move between visible items. Use **→** to expand a group, **←** to collapse it, and **Enter** to open a page.

## Collapsing the sidebar

Use the collapse button beside the site title to give the article more space. The expand button brings the navigation back.
