This site is a starting point for documentation. Fumadocs provides the content, page tree, and search; [M3E](https://github.com/matraic/m3e) web components provide a Material 3 interface with light and dark themes, accessible colour roles, and keyboard-friendly navigation.

## Make it yours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the site">Set `appName` and `gitConfig` in `src/lib/shared.ts`, and replace `BrandMark` in `src/components/ui/brand-icons.tsx` with your logo.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the colours">Change the brand values in `src/styles/theme.css` and the matching roles in `src/styles/color-roles.css`. Keep text at 4.5:1 and indicators at 3:1.</Step>
  <Step title="Configure navigation">Edit the navbar links and menus in `src/lib/layout.shared.tsx`.</Step>
  <Step title="Write your docs">Replace the sample pages in `content/docs`. Every component below works in MDX without imports.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep the samples for reference">
  The example pages show every component and layout option. Delete `content/docs/test` and `content/docs/versions` when you no longer need them.
</Callout>
